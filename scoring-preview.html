How the leaderboard would change under the new scoring

I applied the new scoring to every September post. This is a shadow of how the leaderboard would look under it. It runs next to the live board, so nothing on the live board or in payouts has changed. I am reviewing it first.

Why change it

The current scoring rewards clean writing, good structure and showing you read about a feature. An AI does all three for free: give it a Somnia announcement and it writes a polished explainer in seconds, and the current scoring called that substantive.

What the new scoring does

The judge is shown what Somnia's official accounts have posted recently, and scores your post on what it adds beyond that.

  • Scores low: rewording, summarising or translating an official post, and generic replies.
  • Scores well: your own use, your own numbers, a claim you stake, a specific criticism or bug.

It is not AI detection. It stops paying for the part a tool can do for you.
