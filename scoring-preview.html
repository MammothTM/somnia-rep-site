How the leaderboard would change under the new scoring

Every September post has been scored a second time under a new scoring. This page shows how the leaderboard would look under it. Nothing on the live board or in payouts has changed. I am reviewing the results before deciding.

Why

Many of you said the board was filling up with AI-written posts. The current scoring rewards clean writing and good structure. A tool can produce both in seconds from a Somnia announcement, so those posts scored as well as real work.

How it works

The judge reads what Somnia's official accounts posted before your post, then scores your post on what it adds beyond that.

It is not AI detection. Nobody is accused of anything. It stops paying for the part a tool can do for you.

What scores well

  • Your own use: a trade, a screenshot, your own numbers.
  • A claim, prediction or comparison you stand behind.
  • Specific criticism, a bug, a question that asks for proof.
  • A faithful translation earns a middle score: more than a reworded post, less than original work.

Rewording or summarising an announcement scores low, however polished.
